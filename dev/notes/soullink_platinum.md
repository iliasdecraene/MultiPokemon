# Soul Link helpers — Pokémon Platinum (verified)

Platinum US rev 0 (SHA1 `ce81046e…`, DS, py-desmume). Code: `client/yellowlink/platinum.py` (section "Soul Link" in
`PlatinumMemory` + module helpers), area table `client/yellowlink/platinum_areas.py` (generated by
`dev/platinum/gen_soullink_areas.py`), unit tests `client/tests/test_soullink_platinum.py`, real-ROM checks
`dev/platinum/soullink_verify.py` (ends with `ALL PASS`; sections can be run alone). pret/pokeplatinum has no
symbols: every struct offset below was taken from the decomp and then found/confirmed in the running game.
Everything is heap-allocated, so all of it is reached through pointers (see dev/platinum/MEMORY_MAP.md).

## battle_kind() / battle_info()

* Both come from the DTO, so they are right from the **first frame the battle app exists** and stay until its last
  frame (6 wild battles in a row: every frame equal to the first, equal to the BattleContext's enemy mon once it is
  loaded, never the previous battle's mon; `None` on every frame before the battle app). No frame-1 wait like Crystal.
* Classification (`battle_kind_of(bt)`): special = LINK, FRONTIER (Battle Tower/Frontier), PAL_PARK, CATCH_TUTORIAL,
  DEBUG, and TRAINER+AI (trainer battles with a partner: Cheryl, Mira, Riley, Marley, Buck); trainer = other
  TRAINER battles (incl. two enemy trainers at once, BATTLE_TYPE_TAG|DOUBLES without a partner); wild = the rest:
  grass/surf/fishing/honey tree/scripted statics & legendaries (all BATTLE_TYPE_WILD_MON = 0), roamers (0x100), the
  Great Marsh (SAFARI 0x20) and wild doubles while walking with a partner (AI_PARTNER = 0x4A, no trainer bit).
* dict: `kind, dex` (national dex = Gen-4 species id), `shiny` (Gen-4 rule with the wild mon's own OT ids — the game
  gives wild mons the player's TID/SID), `safari` (Great Marsh), `contest` False, `level`; extras `battle_type`,
  `pid`, `key` (the mon_key the mon has once caught — verified equal on a real catch), `map` (DTO mapHeaderID),
  `others` (species of the other wild mon(s) of a double), `keys` / `dexes` (lists for ALL wild mons of the
  battle, first = `key` / `dex`; verified against battlers 1 and 3 of the real double with Cheryl; a trainer
  battle lists only its first mon).
* If a wild mon's DTO record is unreadable on some frame (being worked on), battle_info() returns None for that
  frame instead of reporting the next mon (never seen: the DTO copies are only touched while the battle starts).
* **Wild doubles (partner)**: `dex` = the first wild mon (battler 1); `others` = [battler 3]. The player can catch
  the second one instead → match the catch on `keys` (a caught mon keeps its key).
* Trainer battles: `dex` = the trainer's first mon (DTO party), not the one currently out.
* Catching tutorial (Dawn, Route 202, real): kind special, dex BIDOOF, battle_party_slot() None on every frame.
* The first rival battle (Barry, Route 201) is a TRAINER battle with BATTLE_STATUS_FIRST_BATTLE (pret).

## End of a battle (timing)

The battle runs on **copies** of the party (DTO/BattleSystem). The save party is updated by the encounter task
(FieldBattleDTO_UpdateFieldSystem) **2 frames after** the battle app ends (= battle_info() turns None) — measured on
a catch and on a blackout. The referee reads the party END_FRAMES = 8 frames later: fine. A catch with a full party
goes straight into the PC (the battle writes PCBoxes directly, into the current box if it has room, else the first box
with room, which then becomes the current box) — verified: party stays 6, `current_box_records()[-1]` = the catch.
Walking with a partner: if the battle is won the game heals the whole party (Party_HealAllMembers) before the
referee reads it — faints there are only seen live (battle_party_slot).

## battle_party_slot()

`(save-party index, current battle HP)` of the player's battler, or None. Read from BattleContext.battleMons[0/2]
(personality, curHP) + selectedPartySlot; the battle party's mon in that slot must have the same PID (raw first word,
works while the battle has it decrypted in place) and the index returned is the position of that PID in the **save**
party (so a Frontier-style party copy in another order can't point at the wrong mon).
* None before the battle mon is loaded (frame ~46 of a wild battle; the context is zeroed at creation), outside
  battles, and for LINK/FRONTIER/PAL_PARK/CATCH_TUTORIAL/SAFARI/DEBUG battles.
* Faint (real, lead at 1 HP, WITHDRAW until the wild mon hits): `(0, 1)` … then `(0, 0)` on consecutive frames.
* Switch-in after the faint: `(0, 0)` → None for exactly 1 frame (selectedPartySlot is written before battleMons) →
  `(1, 16)`; never `(1, 0)`.
* Doubles: battlers 0 and 2 are checked; a battler at HP 0 is preferred (so the second mon's faint is reported too);
  the partner's mons never match the save party (verified with Cheryl's mon at battler 2).
* After the battle the save party has the fainted mon at HP 0 (copy-back).

## Blackout / whiteout

Real: lone lead faints → battle ends → party copied back with HP 0 two frames after battle_info() turns None →
map changes to the Pokémon Center 6 frames later → the heal happens ~540 frames after the battle (COMMON_SCRIPTS
20/21 after the warp). So the referee's `_battle_end` (8 frames) sees HP 0 for the whole party, like Yellow/Crystal.
Not verified: **poison fainting in the overworld** (Gen 4 mons can faint from poison outside battle:
field_control.c Field_UpdatePoison → FLDPSN_FAINTED → common script; a whole-party poison faint whites out inside
that script). The player is never `is_safe()` between that faint and the heal, so a referee that only reads the
party while safe misses a poison whiteout; a single poison faint (others alive) is seen as HP 0 afterwards.

## has_poke_balls()

Any item 1-16 with qty > 0 in the Poké Balls pocket, or FLAG_SAFARI_GAME_ACTIVE (Safari Balls are a counter in
FieldOverworldState, not a bag item). Verified: empty pocket in pt_pc → False; Dawn's real gift; safari battle with
an empty pocket → True.

## Hall of Fame

* `in_hall_of_fame()` = map 187. Verified with the real thing: the warp trick into map 187 runs its OnFrame script
  (Rowan + Cynthia, PlayHallOfFameHealingAnimation, ClearGame, HoF screen = child app overlay 86, in-game save,
  credits = overlay 99). in_game() and in_hall_of_fame() stay True through all of it.
* `entered_hall_of_fame()` = FLAG_GAME_COMPLETED or TrainerInfo.isMainStoryCleared or hall_of_fame_count() > 0 — all
  set by ClearGame during the scene, before the game saves. Reset during the credits + CONTINUE: all still set (the
  save happens before the credits), player back in the bedroom (map 415).
* `hall_of_fame_count()` = GameRecords RECORD_TIMES_ENTERED_HALL_OF_FAME (decrypted; 0 → 1). Returns 0 while the
  records are being re-encoded (byte-sum check). The HoF entries themselves live in a separate flash block, not in RAM.

## Current box + forced moves

* `current_box_records()`: mons of PCBoxes.currentBoxID as encrypted 136-byte records, **slot order, empty slots
  left out** (`platinum_pc.normalise`: records decrypted in place right now are returned as the game will write them
  back; unreadable ones are skipped). `move_box_to_party(i)` takes an index into that list.
* `move_party_to_box(slot)` = the box UI's STORE (PCBoxes_TryStoreBoxMonInBox + Party_RemovePokemonBySlotIndex):
  box part with PP restored (MoveTable_CalcMaxPP with PP Ups) and Shaymin → Land Form, into the first empty slot of
  the current box; party shifted up, last slot = Pokemon_Init (EMPTY_PARTY), count − 1. If the current box is full it
  goes to the next box with room, which becomes the current box (like a catch). Refuses (nothing written): bad slot,
  party not readable this frame (`party_checked()` None), no OTHER non-egg mon with HP > 0, Mail held, Ball Capsule
  attached, all boxes full. **Verified byte for byte** against the game's own STORE (party slots 1, 3, 6 from the
  same state: party page, count, all 18 boxes, current box identical).
* `move_box_to_party(i)` = WITHDRAW (Pokemon_FromBoxPokemon = `pk4.to_party`, Party_AddPokemon, BoxPokemon_Init).
  **Verified byte for byte** against the game's WITHDRAW (box slots 1 and 3) **except 3 × u16**: the Easy-Chat
  sentence ids of the empty mail (plain party offsets 0xBE/0xC6/0xCE). EasyChatSentence_Init never sets `id`, so the
  game leaves heap garbage there (seen 0x157, 0x12B, 0x36/0x9C); `pk4.to_party` writes 0. The game never reads them
  (mail type NONE). The game's own WITHDRAW takes back a mon we deposited.
* Both need `PlatinumMemory(bus, rom)` / `pm.rom = ndsrom.PlatinumData` (move PP, base stats, growth) — the app sets
  `game.rom = plat_data`.
* **Cross-frame stability:** `party_checked()`'s "two reads agree" happens inside one paused DS frame, so it can't
  see an operation the game is still in the middle of. `observe_stable()` records (party via party_checked, current
  box id, the current box's 30 raw slots) with the emulator's frame counter (`NDSCore.frame_count` / `ds.DS.frame`)
  and returns True when it equals the observation from an EARLIER frame. Both forced moves call it first and return
  False ("try again later", nothing written) unless the state was already seen identical on an earlier frame — by a
  previous `observe_stable()` (the referee's view should call it in its safe() check) or a previous attempt of the
  move. Real game: an unobserved first attempt and a second one on the same frame write nothing; the move then went
  through on a later frame (with a 6-mon party whose lead is decrypted in place on about half the frames).
* Stats use the mon's **form** (`pk4.calc_stats` → `PlatinumData.form_base_stats`; pret Pokemon_GetFormNarcIndex:
  Deoxys forms 1-3 → personal 496-498, Wormadam 1-2 → 499-500, Giratina Origin → 501, Shaymin Sky → 502, Rotom 1-5 →
  503-507; values checked against the ROM's personal NARC, which has 508 entries). Before, a withdrawn Rotom
  appliance / Giratina-O / Deoxys form got its base form's stats. Call only while `is_safe()`; Gen-4 withdrawing heals (dead mons must be tracked by key).
* **Mid-frame decryption (important):** in the overworld the game decrypts the lead of a 6-mon party in place
  on ~half of the frames (60/120 measured), sometimes **only the 100-byte party section** — the box checksum is
  still valid then, so a checksum check alone reads garbage HP/level. `party_checked()` now also requires every
  party section to decrypt to plausible values (level 1-100, 0 < max HP, HP ≤ max HP, mail type valid)
  (`party_section_ok`); with it all 60 clean frames gave identical keys/HP/levels. Forced moves only write after a
  clean `party_checked()`.

## Day Care (Solaceon)

`daycare_records()`: [slot 1, slot 2] box records (only non-empty slots); **None** if a slot reads "bad"
(being decrypted right now, or corrupt) — "unknown, skip this scan", never a silently shorter list (unit-tested;
the game only touches the slots when it adds step exp). Real, via the warp trick into map 437 and the
Day-Care Lady: both deposits read back in order, each byte-identical to the party record's first 136 bytes
(BoxPokemon_FromPokemon; no PP restore) with the same key. Taking the first one back (after 4 steps: exp 57 → 61)
gives the same key in the party and the other mon moves to slot 1 (Daycare_ShiftMonSlots). Day Care mons may hold
Mail (stored in DaycareMail).

## mon_key / mon_info

`mon_key(rec)` = `pk4.mon_key` = PID + TID + SID (16 hex digits), for box/party/day-care records, encrypted, plain or
decrypted in place (it can't fix a record caught half re-encrypted: use records from party_checked() /
current_box_records() / daycare_records()). Verified stable: catch (= battle_info key), PC deposit/withdraw (game
and ours), Day Care in/out, a real level-up evolution TURTWIG → GROTLE (Lv 18), every clean overworld frame.
Hatching keeps PID and OT ID (pret Egg_CreateHatchedMonInternal; only a shiny Manaphy egg from Pokémon Ranger gets a
new PID) — verified on a real hatch (below). Gifts get the player's TID/SID (Bebe's EEVEE, real). Collisions need equal 32-bit PIDs among the player's
own mons: negligible. `mon_info(rec)` (Platinum extra) = {key, dex, hp (party; −1 box), egg, nick, level} or None
(empty/unreadable).

## Gift Pokémon / eggs

**Eggs, real:** the Day Care man's egg (RAM: Daycare.offspringPersonality = 0x12345678 at body+0x1654+0x1D8 = "egg
waiting"; talking to him runs the game's egg creation) is a level-1 party record with HP 12/12 → it passes
`party_section_ok()`, `party_checked()` is clean on ~half the frames as usual (the lead's in-place decryption),
`mon_info()['egg']` True, key = 12345678 + the player's TID/SID. Hatch (RAM: the egg's cycles 0, Day Care step
counter 254 at +0x1DC): the game's hatch scene runs on the next step; the hatched BIDOOF has the same key.

Real gift (warp trick into Bebe's house, Hearthome, map 115, from a 5-mon party): EEVEE Lv20 appears in the party
during the script, `battle_info()` is None throughout, the player stands in area `hearthome_city`, key final at once.
So the referee's "new mon outside battle = gift in the current area" works. The **starter** is given by script on
**Route 201** (map 342) right before Barry's first battle (pret scripts_route_201.s: GivePokemon, then the rival
battle) → with the current referee the starter is Route 201's encounter (same for both players, so they are linked);
fossils (Mining Museum), Togepi egg, Riolu egg (Iron Island), Porygon (Veilstone house) count for the town/route the
building is in (see Areas).

## Areas (AREAS, area_of, area_name)

All 593 map headers, generated from pret `generated/map_headers.txt` + `include/data/map_headers.h` (location label
of each map = the ROM's sMapHeaders, checked by verify.py), names from the ROM's location_names bank. The area is the
location label (so every floor of Mt. Coronet, Iron Island, Victory Road, Turnback Cave, Galactic HQ, Old Chateau,
Eterna Forest in/out, Great Marsh 1-6, Route 204/205/210/211/212 halves are one area each, gates belong to their
route), with buildings that have their own label counted in the town/route they stand in (Jubilife TV, Pokétch Co.,
Global Terminal, Trainers' School → Jubilife City; Canalave Library; Mining Museum → Oreburgh City; Cycle Shop,
T.G. Eterna Bldg, ROTOM's Room → Eterna City; Poffin House, Contest Hall, Foreign Building → Hearthome City; Game
Corner, Veilstone Store → Veilstone City; Sunyshore Market, Vista Lighthouse → Sunyshore City; Flower Shop →
Floaroma Town; Pokémon Day Care → Solaceon Town; Pokémon Mansion → Route 212; Footstep House + Grand Lake hotel →
Route 213; Café → Route 210; Restaurant + Grand Lake houses → Valor Lakefront; Villa → Resort Area; Battleground →
Survival Area), the **Lost Tower** separate from Route 209 (own wild Pokémon, like Crystal's Team Rocket Base), and
each "Mystery Zone" map (Underground, Union Room, Colosseums, unused maps) its own area. 131 areas. Keys are at most
37 characters (the server accepts `^[A-Za-z0-9_*-]{1,40}$` and the referee appends `*n` for shiny-clause catches;
the generator refuses longer keys — map 352 is `unused_eterna_gate_route_206`). Names not in the
ROM (Lost Tower, Underground, …) are written in the generator. `location_of(map)` keeps the plain label.
`area_name()` strips the shiny-clause `*n` suffix.
